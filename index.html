Our Story

Started in one classroom. Built for parents who ask around first.

Our Odisha network is a proud franchise of Mastermind Abacus, a Multinational Education Company with a presence across 16 countries and over 2000 associate franchisees worldwide. Locally, it began the way most trusted programmes do, with a handful of children, a soroban, and a teacher who refused to run a batch of more than twelve.

Years on, the batch size hasn't moved. What has changed is the number of children who've sat down nervous about numbers and left a term later reaching for harder problems on their own. That's the only metric that mattered when we started, and it's the only one that matters now.

Today we run Mastermind Abacus's structured, age-wise programmes across Odisha, still taught by people who know each child's name, not just their batch number.

Questions

What parents ask before their first visit.

Five is the earliest we start, and eight is not too late to begin the Foundation level. What matters more than age is whether a child can sit through a focused session. We assess that for free before any enrolment.

Most parents notice a change in focus and homework speed within two terms. Mental calculation without the physical frame typically follows by the second year, depending on attendance and practice at home.

Abacus is a hands-on skill. A teacher needs to see exactly where each child's bead technique breaks down. Above twelve, that correction becomes generic. We've never raised the cap, even when it meant opening a second batch instead.

Thirty minutes, one to one with a teacher. We check number sense, concentration span, and comfort with the soroban, then tell you honestly which level your child should start at, with no obligation to enrol.
